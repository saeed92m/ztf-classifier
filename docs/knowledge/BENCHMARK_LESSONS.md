# Benchmark Lessons

1. Benchmark manifests are immutable versioned contracts for source, population, labels, preprocessing, split, leakage exclusions, and provenance.
2. Benchmark input hashes must be retained with reports.
3. Regression remains release-blocking until dispositioned.
4. Missing or non-executable scientific evidence is never silently treated as pass.
5. Each benchmark execution should emit scientific results and reusable lessons/failure patterns.
6. Benchmark population, source version, and retrieval date are evidence, not incidental metadata.


7. Prefer immutable publisher snapshots for historical reproduction when available; use live services for drift monitoring.
8. Published membership and current-source derivation are distinct evidence products and must have separate acceptance criteria.
9. A benchmark contract is not benchmark evidence; the archive must be acquired, hashed, schema-checked, and executed.
10. Source-specific preprocessing and feature-name mappings are part of reproducibility and must be versioned.
11. Historical data drift is a scientific finding and must not be “fixed” by weakening assertions.
